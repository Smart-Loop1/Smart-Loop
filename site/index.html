The story

From a hidden problem to a visible signal.

The problem

Leaks and unusual consumption often remain unnoticed until damage appears or the monthly bill arrives.

Our solution

Live flow readings make unusual use visible from the first moment, while goals, cost estimates, analytics, and reports keep everything understandable.

Built to be useful

A clearer view of every litre.

01

Live flow monitoring

See the current flow rate, total consumption, and device status as readings arrive.

02

Early leak awareness

Spot unusual consumption while it is happening instead of waiting for the next bill.

03

Usage goals

Set a daily or monthly target in litres or estimated cost and follow progress visually.

04

Cost estimation

Translate water usage into an estimated residential cost using Saudi tariff tiers.

05

Analytics

Review consumption trends across daily, monthly, and yearly views.

06

Shareable reports

Generate clean PDF reports that turn usage history into a clear summary.

The application

Designed around the reading that matters now.

The interface brings live device information, goals, and longer-term analytics into one focused experience.
